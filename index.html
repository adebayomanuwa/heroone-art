The journey

From the ground up.

A decade-plus arc from early records to independent ownership, global catalog management, and a philosophy of its own.

  1. 2010

    Klaplyne Records

    The first imprint. Where the foundation was poured.

  2. 2011–16

  3. 2016

    MGN Workaholix Entertainment Ltd

    Formalizing the business of sound.

  4. 2017–20

    Expanding the reach and sharpening the craft.
