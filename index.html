Music / Press releaseHero One · 2026

LDCAT&Highest Currency, Gotta Get it

LDCAT was mastered in a coffee shop in downtown London, Ontario, but began in Lagos in 2023, originally as Outside. Its identity passed through different periods of my life, with arrangements taking shape in 2025 and completion coming in 2026.

Meanwhile, the musical origins of “Highest Currency, Gotta Get it” stretch back through Massive Gang Nation, Lagos and Osogbo, Osun State. The new hook formulation draws from that history. LDCAT can be Latin/Reggaeton. Highest Currency can have an entirely different musical architecture. Both significantly grounded in “Beats & Blues”.

The journey

From the ground up.

A decade-plus arc from early records to independent ownership, global catalog management, and a philosophy of its own.

  1. 2010

    Klaplyne Records

    The first imprint. Where the foundation was poured.

  2. 2011–16

    Massive Gang Nation

    Movement-building years, first stages and a first award. ReverbNation

  3. 2016

    MGN Workaholix Entertainment Ltd

    Formalizing the business of sound.

  4. 2017–20

    Music Genius Network

    Expanding the reach and sharpening the craft.

  5. 2020

    Millenial Renaissance Art Limited

    Full ownership of the masters and the message.

  6. 2022

    Opulence Grandeur Sound

    The sonic imprint for catalog distribution and sync.

  7. 2024

    1000 BOTTLES

    EPs M.C, O.T 3, BAYO, Lifted, and the moments around Force and DFDDP. LIMITS, from 1000 BOTTLES: “All actions and desires are circumscribed within limits.”

  8. 2025

    Heard in other languages

    Press in English, French and Spanish, and radio support for Faa ji. See the portfolio

  9. 2026

    Variety in Observable Consciousness

    The idea in the music becomes a stated law, first stated 20 October 2026. Read the law

“The observer, observation, observable, even ‘awareness’ and eventual expression all are circumscribed within TIME, choice, alteration and environment.”
Philosophy · The Law

Variety in Observable Consciousness.

Everything involved in seeing and knowing anything happens inside four conditions: time, the choices made, the changes that occur, and the surroundings. Nothing gets a seat outside, not even awareness itself. First stated 20 October 2026, with the full record of its first twelve stress tests.
