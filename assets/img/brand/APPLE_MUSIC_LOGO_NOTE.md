# Apple Music artist logo

**File:** `HeroOne_AppleMusic_Logo_3000x1200.png`
**Source:** `heroone-logo.webp` (1200 × 735, the gold Hero One wordmark)

## What it meets
- PNG with a transparent background, no border
- 3000 × 1200 canvas (Apple's logo template size)
- Logo fills the full 960 px minimum height (1568 × 960), centered
- Gold, bright color with its own dark bevel outline, so it reads over both dark and light artist images
- Text is only the artist name

## How it was made
- Trimmed to the logo's edges, scaled up 1.31× with Lanczos resampling on premultiplied alpha (no dark halos), then lightly sharpened
- Checked at 100% on dark and light backgrounds: edges clean, no pixelation

## Upload
Apple Music for Artists → Artist Content → Artist Logo → Choose Logo → pick this file.

If Apple ever needs more resolution than this, the best fix is a vector (SVG/AI) export of the wordmark, so it can be rendered at any size.

Spec reference: https://musicconnect.apple.com/support/5610-artist-logo-guidelines
