```html
<p>Adebayo Michael Manuwa, famously known as Hero One. Originator of “Beats &amp; Blues”.</p><p>His music was born in Lagos and lives with him in Toronto. To him, music is more than sound: “It’s a mirror to real life, and a vessel for emotion, culture, and purpose. Every track is crafted to heal, reflect, and rise.”</p><p>He thinks for himself and builds for himself. He owns his masters and his publishing, designs his own artwork, and doesn’t wait for anyone’s permission to create.</p><p>Behind the music is a belief he has written down as a law: all of us, the artist, the listener, even our awareness, are shaped by time, choice, change and our surroundings. No one hears from nowhere. That’s why a song can mean something different to you today than it did yesterday, and that’s the point.</p><p>Heal. Reflect. Rise.</p>
```

Opening line is Hero One's own wording, verbatim.
