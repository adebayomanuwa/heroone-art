# YouTube video watermark

**Use:** `HeroOne_YouTube_Watermark_Bolt_800.png` (recommended)
**Alt:** `HeroOne_YouTube_Watermark_Wordmark_800.png`

Both are taken from the cleaned Hero One logo (no dots or connectors), the same artwork as the Apple Music logo.

## Why the bolt
YouTube shows the watermark at roughly 60–120 px in the corner of the player. At that size the H-bolt mark stays clear, while the full "Hero One" wordmark gets too small to read. The wordmark version is there in case you still want it.

## Specs met
- Square, 800 × 800 PNG (YouTube needs at least 150 × 150 and under 1 MB; each file is about 105 KB)
- Transparent background, gold with its own dark outline so it reads over light and dark footage

## Upload
YouTube Studio → Customization → Branding → Video watermark → Upload → choose the display time (end of video, custom start time, or entire video) → Publish.

Spec reference: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525
